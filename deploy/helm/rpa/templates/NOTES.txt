Before install:
- Create the Secret named {{ .Values.secrets.existingSecret }} (default: rpa-runtime-secrets) from an external SecretStore or platform secret manager.
- Apply db/roles.sql out-of-band and provision rpa_migrator, rpa_app, and rpa_lifecycle_bypass credentials.
- Run the rpa-migrate Job before allowing API/worker readiness.
- Keep ingress.enabled=false until the platform owner supplies className, host, TLS secret, and matching NetworkPolicy source approval.
- Default egress is deny-all plus DNS. Populate networkPolicy.egress.ownerApprovedCidrs only with owner-approved destination CIDRs and ports for PostgreSQL, Vault, object storage, OTLP, and LLM providers.
